Which Phone Plan Should You Pick? Vodafone vs Telstra vs Optus

Medha Holla · Customer guide · Prices checked 12 October 2026

Summary. I compared 9 SIM-only plans from Australia's three big carriers and worked out the best-value choice for four kinds of customer.

Student: Vodafone Student Small (200GB, $39). Light user / senior: Optus Small ($39) in the city, Telstra Basic if you live regionally. Heavy user: Vodafone Large (420GB, $55). Traveller / calls family overseas: Vodafone Medium ($50).

1. The plans compared

2. What actually matters when choosing

  • How much data you really use. Check Settings → Mobile Data on your phone. Most people use 10–40GB a month, so the biggest plan is often wasted money.
  • The price after the first year. Promo prices jump after 12 months. Vodafone Large goes from $55 to $78.
  • Coverage where you live. In the city all three work well. In regional areas Telstra generally has the widest coverage.
  • Overseas use. Look at roaming costs and international calls, not just the data.

3. Best pick for each customer

Student on a budget

Pick: Vodafone Student Small, 200GB for $39/mth for the first year. Students get triple the data of the normal Small plan for less money. Optus has a similar student offer (200GB for $39), so either is great. Just bring your student ID.

Light user or senior (mostly calls, some browsing)

Pick: Optus Small, $39/mth with 100GB for the first year. It's the cheapest plan here and more data than a light user needs. If they live outside a major city, choose Telstra Basic ($64/mth), because reliable coverage matters more than saving $25 a month.

Heavy user (streams video, uses hotspot)

Pick: Vodafone Large, 420GB for $55/mth for the first year. It's the cheapest data of all 9 plans, at just 13 cents per GB, with more data than Telstra's $99 Premium plan for about half the price.

Traveller or someone who calls family overseas

Pick: Vodafone Medium, 220GB for $50/mth for the first year. It includes unlimited standard international calls to Zone 1 countries, and Vodafone's $5-a-day roaming lets you use your normal plan data overseas. The Optus Plus plan is the runner-up ($69, unlimited calls to 35 countries, $5 for 5GB a day roaming).

4. Questions I'd ask a customer before recommending

  1. How much data do you use now? (Let's check your phone together.)
  2. Where do you live and travel? Is coverage important there?
  3. Do you travel overseas or call family in another country?
  4. Are you a student, or eligible for a concession?
  5. What's your budget after the first-year discount ends?

5. Things to watch out for

  • Promo prices are for new customers and have end dates: Vodafone's ends 29 October 2026, Optus Small's 12 November 2026, and Telstra's needs the code by 2 November 2026.
  • Prices change often. Always check the carrier's Critical Information Summary before signing up.
